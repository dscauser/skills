# Branch: personal

For everyday ideas and decisions: home, money, health and fitness, learning,
hobbies, travel, side projects, career moves and life changes.

Tone: warmer and lighter than the work branches. Still honest, but this is the
user's life, so they decide; you help them see it clearly.

## Sharpen: what to find out

- What is pulling them towards this? Excitement, a problem, someone else's
  suggestion?
- What does a good outcome look like in three months, and in a year?
- What is the real budget: money, time per week, energy?
- Who else does it affect?

## Thinking moves (for Widen)

Use two or three that fit.

- **Try it small.** Rent before buying, a taster class before a course, a
  weekend before a move, a month before a habit.
- **Future you.** How will they feel about this in a year if they do it? If
  they do not?
- **The underlying want.** What is the idea really for (rest, status, health,
  connection, money)? Is there an easier way to get that?
- **Undo cost.** How easy is it to reverse? Cheap-to-undo ideas deserve a
  faster yes.
- **Borrow experience.** Who do they know who has done this? What would that
  person say?
- **Combine.** Could it fold into something they already do?

## Pressure test

- What is the most likely way this fizzles out, and what would prevent it?
- Hidden costs: upkeep, time, knock-on spending, effect on others.
- Is the timing right, or is there a better moment?
- For money decisions: stay general and label estimates. Do not give
  personalised financial, medical or legal advice; suggest a qualified
  professional where it matters.

## Trim targets

The smallest version is usually a dated, low-cost trial with one thing to
notice at the end ("after four weekends, do I still look forward to it?").

## Brief extras

Add: the underlying want, the trial, the real budget, and the check-in date.
Keep the brief short and friendly.

## Hand on

Suggest a simple first step they can take this week. Only suggest other skills
or tools if the idea turned into a project (for example the build branch).
