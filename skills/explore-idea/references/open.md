# Branch: open

For ideas that fit no other branch, or that are still just a hunch: community
projects, events, creative work, research questions, "something feels off about
X", or a theme the user wants to explore without a goal yet.

Spend longer in Sharpen and Widen here. Often the main result is finding out
which other branch the idea belongs in; switch to it when that becomes clear.

## Sharpen: what to find out

- Finish the sentence: "It would be great if…"
- Is this a problem to fix, an opportunity to grab, or a curiosity to explore?
- Who would care about it besides the user?
- What would make this conversation worthwhile even if nothing happens next?

## Words to pin down

Common sources of talking past each other. Check the ones the idea uses.

- The key noun of the idea: ask what it means to the user before anything else.
- Any word doing a lot of work ("better", "community", "simple"): better how, for whom?

## Thinking moves (for Widen)

Use two or three that fit.

- **Five whys.** Ask why it matters until the real goal appears.
- **Different scales.** What does it look like for one person, for a hundred,
  for everyone?
- **Analogy.** Where has something like this worked in a completely different
  field?
- **Constraint flip.** What if it had to be done for free? In a day? With no
  technology?
- **Opposite.** What would the reverse idea be, and is that interesting too?
- **Who else.** Who is already working on this, and could the user join them
  instead of starting from scratch?

## Pressure test

- Is there a clear enough goal to judge it against? If not, agree one before
  giving a verdict.
- What is the cost of trying, and what is the cost of not trying?
- What is the single biggest unknown, and how could it be checked cheaply?

## Trim targets

The smallest version is usually a question to answer or a small experiment to
run, not a plan.

## Brief extras

Add: the goal agreed in Sharpen, the biggest unknown, and the experiment that
checks it. If the idea moved to another branch, say which.

## Hand on

Point to the branch the idea now fits, or suggest the one experiment to run
before coming back.
