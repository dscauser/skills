# PDF design context (optional)

A filled-in copy of this file lets every PDF follow your brand or your own
taste. Copy it to `context.md` next to this skill's SKILL.md (applies to
every project), or to `pdf-context.md` in a project folder (that project
only, and it wins over `context.md`). In the skills repo, `context.md` and
a `brand/` folder next to it (for logo and font files) are git-ignored.
A `pdf-context.md` belongs to its project: commit it if the team shares it.

Fill in only what you care about and delete the rest. Leave a field out
rather than guessing. No passwords or confidential data.

- Name as it should appear: <e.g. Acme Ltd, or your own name>
- Logo file: <e.g. brand/logo.svg; SVG keeps it vector in the PDF>
- Where the logo goes: <e.g. cover top left, never on inside pages>

## Colour

- Accent: <hex> (<role, e.g. one highlight per page, chart emphasis>)
- Neutrals: <hex for body text>, <hex for muted text>, <hex for tints>
- Chart colours, in order: <hex, hex, hex>
- Colours to avoid: <e.g. red except for losses>

## Type
- Headings: <font name, and file in brand/ if not a system font>
- Body: <font name>
- Fallback: <e.g. Segoe UI, Arial>

## Look and feel
- Three words for the style: <e.g. calm, precise, warm>
- Principles: <e.g. lots of white space; one chart per page; no stock photos>
- Never: <e.g. gradients, rounded cards, all-caps labels>
- Examples you like: <file paths or URLs of documents to match>

## Page defaults
- Paper: <A4 or Letter>
- Footer text: <e.g. "Acme Ltd | Confidential">
- Page numbers: <e.g. "3 of 12", bottom right, not on the cover>
- Classification line: <e.g. "Internal" on every page, or none>

## Voice
- Spelling: <UK or US English>
- Tone: <e.g. plain and direct; no exclamation marks>
- Words to use or avoid: <e.g. "customers", never "users">
